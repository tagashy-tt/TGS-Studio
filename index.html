About TGS Studios

TGS Studios is a solo development operation focused on creator tools and community infrastructure. Every project is designed, built, and maintained by me — TAGASHY, an independent developer and anime/gaming editor.

The work spans Discord and WhatsApp bots, After Effects and Chrome extensions, a mobile app, and web platforms, with a recurring focus on TikTok tooling, ffmpeg pipelines, and anime content communities.

It's not a big team — it's a commitment to shipping things that actually work, with real users behind every project.
